Our foundation, vision, and the core values that drive our global growth.

Company Background & Founding Story

Veyronyx Innovation Capital was founded with an initial capital base of USD 63 million to support innovation-driven businesses and long-term value creation. Since our inception, our focus has been dedicated to bridging the gap between emerging deep technologies and massive global industrial applications across artificial intelligence, blockchain infrastructure, digital assets, energy technologies, and strategic private investments.

Headquartered in San Francisco, our global growth and expansion strategy seeks out resilient founders worldwide. We believe in creating capital pipelines that foster sustainable, scalable, and resilient growth.
